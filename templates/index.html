My Journey

Three Rock-Bottom Moments
That Made Me

"After living through it all, I've come to one simple truth — life doesn't test you once; it tests you again and again, until you break… or become unbreakable."

01

The Day My Mother Cried Silently

Fresh out of SSC, unable to afford college, I became a factory helper — cleaning metal burrs and oiling machines. My mother visited the workshop and saw me covered in oil and grease, hunched over a steel platform, scrubbing like my life depended on it.

"There's a kind of pain that doesn't come from hunger — it comes from seeing your parent silently suffer for you. That was my first taste of rock bottom. I swore I'd never let her see me like that again."

02

The Fall That Shattered My Comfort Zone

After completing ITI and working at Tata Motors for three years, I fell from a 10-foot platform while welding — without safety equipment. That fall didn't just knock me down. It knocked me awake.

"My colleague Mr. Chatterjee said: 'Tushar, you're meant for more.' No one had ever said that before. I quit my stable job to pursue a diploma in engineering. Everyone thought I was crazy. But I wasn't chasing pride — I was chasing purpose."

03

Drenched, Broken & Still Standing

Jobless, failing engineering exams, relationship ended — I became a food delivery boy in heavy July rain. I climbed 8 floors (elevator broken) to deliver food. The customer abused me and didn't pay. I walked down those stairs with tears I couldn't hold back.

"From that day, I decided — no one, and nothing, will ever make me feel that powerless again. The pain you're going through? It's not the end. It's the foundation."

"The world ain't all sunshine and rainbows. It's a very mean and nasty place... But it ain't about how hard you hit. It's about how hard you can get hit and keep moving forward. That's how winning is done."

— Rocky Balboa
